Proposal 3 - Relationship path

Read With Someone

A private co-reading path where the first meaningful action is choosing an artifact and one person. The prototype tests whether intimacy beats broad discovery for activation.

Hypothesis

People are more likely to understand Highlighter when it starts with a specific thing they care about and a specific person they want to read it with.

Activation

Activation is room creation plus an invite sent with a concrete reading prompt, before account setup, profile polishing, or community browsing.

Viral Loop

The invite carries the artifact, the inviter, and a lightweight notification contract. The recipient joins a private room and can later invite one more reader.

Risk

Too much intimacy can make the product feel like a private messaging feature. The room needs to reveal artifact-centered reading value quickly.
