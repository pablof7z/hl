Proposal 1 - Invite path

Saved Seat

Alex invites the user into a specific reading room, where the first session explains why the room exists, asks for one lightweight reaction, saves a minimal reading identity, and turns that first signal into a careful one-person invite.

Hypothesis

A named invite with a visible seat in a live room will outperform generic onboarding because the user understands who is waiting, what they are reading, and what action is expected before account setup.

Activation

The activation moment is the first reaction to a highlight. It proves the user can contribute to the room without writing a post, importing contacts, or learning Nostr first.

Viral Loop

After the reaction, Highlighter asks for one specific person who would care about the same passage. The invite carries the room, artifact, inviter, and highlighted sentence as context.

Risk

This can feel socially pressuring if the invite copy overstates urgency. The prototype keeps the room small, makes skipping possible, and frames identity save as trust and portability rather than a crypto lesson.
