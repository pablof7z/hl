Proposal 5 - Creator path

Founding Circle

A creator-led onboarding path for people who arrive with the urge to start a small room. The flow keeps the group intentionally narrow: mission, first artifact, privacy, a few people, restrained notifications, then the room opens.

Hypothesis

Some users do not want to browse communities first. They want to gather a handful of people around one clear reading mission. Highlighter should make that feel safer than starting another public feed.

Activation

Activation happens when the user creates a room with a named mission, adds one artifact, and sends three to five intentional invites.

Viral Loop

Each invite carries the room mission plus the first artifact, so recipients understand why they were chosen before they install or sign in.

Risk

The path may overfit confident creators. It must avoid empty-room anxiety and make privacy/notification restraint visible enough that invites feel respectful.
