Proposal 2 · Content path

Sentence First

A cold-start flow that asks for reactions to compelling excerpts before asking for identity. The product learns taste, reveals a starter shelf, opens the artifact behind a saved highlight, then makes account saving feel like preserving work already done.

Hypothesis

People who do not arrive through an invite still understand Highlighter quickly if the first object is a strong sentence, not an empty feed or signup form.

Activation

Activation is the first saved highlight plus one opened artifact. The shelf is earned from reactions, so the user sees immediate personalization before creating an identity.

Viral Loop

A saved passage becomes the share object: "this sentence made me think of you" links back to the artifact, the user's shelf, and the group discussing it.

Risk

If excerpts feel too generic, this becomes another taste quiz. The passage inventory has to be sharp, attributed, and varied enough to feel curated by readers.
