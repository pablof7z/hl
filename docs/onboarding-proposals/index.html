Wireframe proposals - iOS onboarding

Five ways Highlighter can greet people with a reason to stay.

These are interactive iPhone wireframes, not production UI. They test different onboarding theses for a social reading app where most people arrive from a person, an artifact, a highlight, or the urge to invite someone specific.

Proposal 1

Saved Seat

Invite-led onboarding where the user joins a specific person's reading room, reacts to a highlight, then invites someone who would care about the same passage.

 Proposal 2

Sentence First

Cold-start onboarding through excellent excerpts. The user builds taste from passages, then sees the artifacts and communities behind those choices.

 Proposal 3

Read With Someone

The first action is choosing a real artifact and one person to discuss it with. It tests intimate pair/small-room onboarding before broader discovery.

 Proposal 4

Artifact Passport

A newcomer enters a group by bringing one artifact. Their recommendation becomes their introduction and a new invite object.

 Proposal 5

Founding Circle

For users who want to start the room. They define a small mission, add a first artifact, and invite carefully with visible privacy and notification restraint.

Research Inputs

Inputs included Highlighter's product/client specs and iOS implementation audit, multiple isolated agent brainstorms, web research on onboarding and iOS permission timing, `npx skills find onboarding experience`, and a Claude Opus ideation pass. The strongest common pattern: people should accomplish something meaningful before profile, contacts, permissions, or key-management education.
