Proposal 4 - Artifact Passport

Join by bringing one thing worth reading.

An invited newcomer earns their first group moment by adding one artifact, explaining why it belongs, saving a light reading identity, and sending the artifact onward to someone who would recognize it.

Hypothesis

A contribution-first invite will make the group feel legible and participatory before account setup. The artifact becomes both the newcomer's introduction and the reason for the next invite.

Activation

Activated when the user posts one artifact, adds a one-line why, and saves a reading identity that appears on the newcomer shelf.

Viral Loop

The final invite is framed around recognition: send this artifact to the person who would know why it matters, not to a generic contact list.

Risk

The contribution gate may feel too demanding for passive invitees, especially if they arrive without a specific artifact in mind.
