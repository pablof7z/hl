# Communities

## Overview

Highlighter communities are NIP-29 groups with portable user ownership that organize around source content rather than ephemeral posts. Every interaction is designed for virality loops and user-controlled sharing. <!-- [^019f0-e36c8] -->

## Technical Architecture

NIP-29 groups are chosen for communities because they are relay-native, portable, have built-in membership and role semantics, and support the required access×visibility combinations. <!-- [^019f0-a68ff] -->
